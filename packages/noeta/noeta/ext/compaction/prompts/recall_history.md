Recall original messages that compaction collapsed into the summary note at the head of the conversation.

The originals are retained in full — the note merely stands in for them in the prompt. Use this tool when the note lacks a detail you need: the exact text of an earlier error, code you produced or discussed before compaction, or the precise wording of an earlier exchange. The collapsed range is [0, boundary); the current boundary is reported in every result and in the collapsed-context reminder.

Results are a read-only rendering of the original messages; long blocks are truncated with a marker. Page through a large range with repeated calls, advancing `offset`.
